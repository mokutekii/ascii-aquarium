# Keybinds and events

Open **Settings → keys → show** for the in-app list; click an entry to use it. Keys work in either case while the water has focus. Most events also happen on their own. Turn individual events on or off in the gear menu; disabled events ignore their shortcut. Event pace **off** stops random arrivals while leaving enabled shortcuts available.

Ongoing encounters finish when their event is disabled. Asking for an active major event can queue another; creature caps still apply. Activity-crewed submarines follow assistant activity independently of random submarine arrivals.

## A few things to try

- Hammerheads ram hulls; sawsharks cut them. Dragging alien or hijacked UFOs into sharks has the same contact rules as dragging submarines into them.
- UFOs avoid each other, but forced contact can damage both craft. Hijacked UFOs join the defenders and trade fire with alien UFOs, escorts and the mothership.
- The mothership escalates as it takes damage: reserve drones at 50%, faster jumps and rim defenses at 33%, and frequent three-turn sweeps at 10%. Its charged rim blast pushes wildlife, craft and debris outward; small fish are vaporized and heavy creatures resist more.
- The Ghost drifts peacefully until attacked. Its passage gives other titans a gentle wake; it remains larger than the Kraken and Megalodon.
- Shots hit the first body in their path. Explosions can trigger nearby wreckage and drone cores, and defenders can salvage alien armor.

The mothership cannot be grabbed. Its spin sends lasers in all directions, and its rim provokes nearby titans. Above half health, ten seconds without damage lets its alien hull slowly regenerate at 0.8% of maximum health per second; taking damage interrupts it and restarts the delay. Longer encounters draw a larger defensive squad and faster defender fire, within existing population limits. Manual submarine and UFO spawns have no population cap; automatic spawning stays bounded.

Alien salvage gives a diver a faint cyan shield. It absorbs one damaging hit or lethal event, including a bite or hull impact, then breaks. A brief flash prevents one impact from counting twice.

UFOs and submarines each have four hull points. Megalodon body contact deals two damage; surviving UFOs flash red. Kraken catches thrown craft. Its mothership wrap engulfs the hull, then allocates arms between crushing and incoming drone threats. Enraged whale wraps can spare more arms; Megalodon wraps rarely do. Drones sometimes evade a snatch, with much lower odds against an enraged Kraken.

Titan clashes retain committed lunges, jaw-contact mauls, thrashing, escape chases and harpoon reattachment. Missed lunges disengage rather than attaching remotely.
